# An Evening in Vientiane

CCT 360 lab: Soundscape (Part A) and Visualscape (Part B).

## Part A: Soundscape (90 seconds)

A Lao evening, from sunset at the temple to night by the Mekong.

| Time | Scene | Clip | Recorded in | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:20 | Sunset at the temple | [Monks of Wat Sensaikharam - Laos](https://freesound.org/s/179652) by RTB45 | Luang Prabang, Laos | CC BY 4.0 |
| 0:20 to 0:45 | Evening in the village | [Hoybo village, Muang Ngoi Neua](https://freesound.org/people/caquet/sounds/221157/) by caquet | Luang Prabang Province, Laos | CC BY 4.0 |
| 0:45 to 1:10 | A sudden rainstorm | [Rain and thunder in Thailand](https://freesound.org/s/447510) by felix.blume | Krabi, Thailand | CC0 |
| 1:10 to 1:30 | Night by the Mekong | [Crickets in the night](https://freesound.org/s/221164) by caquet | Vang Vieng, Vientiane Province, Laos | CC BY 4.0 |
| 1:10 to 1:30 | (layered on top) | [Mating call of a male Tokay gecko](https://c.enwp.org/wiki/File:Mating_call_of_a_male_Tokay_gecko_(Gekko_gecko).ogg) by Richard Ling | Mekong riverbank near Huay Xai, Laos | Public domain |

## Part B: Visualscape

- The picture and title change by themselves as the soundscape plays (the `timeupdate` event)
- The picture fades between scenes and gently pulses while the audio is playing
- A Play/Pause button, four buttons that jump to each scene, and a volume slider
